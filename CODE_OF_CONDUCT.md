# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want trailguard to be a friendly, respectful place for everyone who uses or contributes to it, whatever their experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to open source or to Express/Prisma.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's identity
- Trolling, spam, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban people who repeatedly or seriously do so.

To report a problem, email **daksheshsharmadev@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
